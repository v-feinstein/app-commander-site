Privacy Policy

Effective November 2026

In short: App Commander has no account, no analytics, and no tracking. Your apps, pages, and settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

The list of apps you add, your pages, their order, sizes and colors, launch counts, and your settings are stored only on your iPhone or iPad. They are not sent to us or to anyone else, and they are removed when you delete the app.

What is sent over the internet

To show an app's name, icon, developer, and category, App Commander asks Apple's public App Store directory. That request contains only what's needed to find the app:

  • the search words you type when adding an app, or
  • the App Store link or app identifier of an app you add.

The app then downloads that app's icon from Apple. These requests go directly to Apple; we don't receive them. Apple's handling of them is covered by Apple's privacy policy.

Shortcuts

To open apps, App Commander uses a shortcut you add to Apple's Shortcuts app. It runs on your device and only passes the identifier of the app you tapped from App Commander to Shortcuts.

What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking, and no third-party SDKs that do these things.
  • We don't read which apps are installed on your device, beyond checking whether a few well-known apps can be opened, to suggest them during setup. That check happens on your device and isn't sent anywhere.
  • We don't sell or share any data, because we don't collect any.

Purchases

If App Commander offers in-app purchases, they're handled entirely by Apple. We never see your payment details.

Children

App Commander doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy: privacy@app-commander.com
