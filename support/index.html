Using App Commander

How do I add apps?

Three ways: check them in → Starter Apps; tap + and search the App Store; or on the Home Screen, long-press any app → Share App → App Commander, and pick the page.

Where are the and + buttons?

They're hidden to keep pages clean. Long-press the page title or any empty spot to customize: the buttons appear, along with controls for icon size, spacing, colors, and title size. Tap Done when finished.

How do I move an app to another page?

Long-press the app's icon → Move to Page, or Copy to Page to keep it in both places. Rearrange pages and their apps in → Pages.

Can each page look different?

Yes. Icon size, spacing, and background and text colors are set per page from the customize panel. The default look for all pages is in → Appearance.

Why isn't “Share App” in the menu for some apps?

iOS only offers it for apps installed from the App Store, so it's missing for built-in apps like Settings and for beta (TestFlight) apps. Use Starter Apps or search instead.

Some apps I have aren't checked in Starter Apps

iOS only lets App Commander confirm a few well-known apps. Anything it can't confirm is under Not detected; check the ones you have.

Opening apps

Why does it need the Shortcuts app?

iOS doesn't allow one app to open another by name; Shortcuts can. App Commander passes the app you tapped to its launch shortcut. Apps that support direct links open without it.

An app doesn't open

  • Check → Shortcut Setup: the status should be green.
  • Make sure the app is still installed on this device.
  • If Shortcuts asked to allow output, choose Always Allow.

I see the Shortcuts app for a moment

For apps without direct links, iOS may briefly show Shortcuts while it opens the app. That's expected.
